## 5. Causal narrative

**CensusIncome (head-side screening).** The protocol branch fixed the fairness substrate (split/model/env seeds decoupled; content-addressed stage-1; true freeze; A/B gates) and produced the single baseline row every later arm cites. Ten arms then probed distinct head-side modifications under a frozen bar (≥ baseline + 0.00203, declared before each run): attention-derived env vectors, a null candidate, conditional scale+bias (with a zero-training router probe), two CGR variants, two affinity variants, a LoRA-style residual, a residual prompt, and a gradient-conflict audit. The pattern was uniform: mechanisms that activate do not move AUC (best +0.0013); mechanisms from the archive are structurally degenerate; the audit finds no action. The one cross-lineage test (importing the AliCCP damping constant) moves the metric the wrong way and lacks the AliCCP mechanism signature. Net: the family's negative is *informative* (mechanism-level, pre-registered, and falsifiable), but it is a stop under this protocol, seed and budget.

**AliCCP (attribution → replication → budget).** The null expert produced a split verdict — utility passed, mechanism failed — which is exactly the case that demands attribution before belief. The constant-damping control explained 91.8% of the gain and, unusually, reproduced the induced head-gate rebalancing. The learnable-scalar branch then showed the training objective itself does **not** discover that operating point from identity (transient 0.55% depth, then parked), implying the benefit requires the specific damping magnitude, not merely "any attenuation". Seed-2 replication held with unrelaxed thresholds (pooled STABLE). The 10-epoch test then separated durable lift from acceleration: per-epoch Δ shrank monotonically and turned negative at ep7 → NOT_PERSIST. In parallel, the clustering repair turned the B4 gate from structural failure to bit-predictable balance on two seeds, but its stage-2 utility direction was seed-unstable (NOT_SUPPORTED). The residual-prompt line repeated this ladder but with a different ending: seed 1 barely under the U1 bar (VALID_NEGATIVE), seed 2 clearly over (REPLICATION_SUPPORTED, but with sign-flipped α and near-zero cos — mechanism path unstable, utility sign stable), and the 10-epoch budget retained the delta (PERSISTS, narrowing from ep4). The divergence between the two AliCCP lines at the same budget/same artifact (attenuation NOT_PERSIST vs residual prompt PERSISTS) is a recorded empirical fact of this screening; it is not explained by the committed evidence. The A3 calibration (item 8) then measured the run-noise dimension for the seed-2 short baseline config: same-config reruns are bit-equal, so the divergence and the narrow U1 miss are not run-noise artifacts under this protocol on this machine; the cross-seed/budget reading is untouched (the calibration explicitly does not cover those dimensions).

**Cross-dataset reading.** The only two direct cross-dataset transports both went to CensusIncome: the attenuation constant (negative, r reversed) and the residual-prompt form (VALID_NEGATIVE there; its AliCCP runs are positive). The null-expert mechanism replicated neither cleanly (each dataset passed exactly one of the two gates). Conclusion: nothing here supports cross-dataset generality claims; AliCCP findings stay AliCCP-specific.

## 6. Novelty and prior-art caution
