## 1. Executive conclusions

1. **Two independent fair-evaluation lineages were built from `master @ fd75198` and everything else was screened on top of them.** CensusIncome: `infra/fair-stage2-benchmark` @ `87afe03` (baseline run `20260929-1735-…-904f8d0`, AUC-Test-Education **0.8500685307175756**). AliCCP: `infra/aliccp-fair-benchmark` @ `8133d32` (seed-1 short baseline `20261003-0130-…-b2e17f9`, AUC-Test-BSI **0.5988392178311113**; seed-2 baseline `20261003-0624-…-79b5e07`, **0.5974422649550507**, recorded on a sibling branch). Every arm ran pre-registered criteria frozen before its run; default paths were guarded bit-identical to the base by tests.

2. **CensusIncome stage-2 head-side screening family: 0 of 10 arms met the pre-registered utility bar** (arm ≥ 0.8521 = baseline + 0.00203). Best result: null expert **+0.0012962965** (mechanism active; still under the bar). Everything else was ≤ +4.9e-5 or negative (from −0.00094 to −0.00355). Two historical archive mechanisms (CGR, affinity gate) were faithfully re-run and found **structurally degenerate** (the affinity eval path provably routes as pure attention for any parameters). Grad-conflict audit returned **NO_ACTION** (mean cos +0.0008, gate needs ≤ −0.05) → no gradient surgery. This family is evidence-based-closed.

3. **AliCCP null-expert line resolved as an attribution story, not a method story.** The null expert passed the utility bar (Δtest **+0.013846535450687258**) but failed its mechanism gate (`null_top1_rate = 0.0`); the null weights collapsed to a **near-constant damping ×0.697** (null_mean 0.3027766269669533, std 0.0085). The post-hoc constant-damping control (c = 0.6972233730330467, zero new parameters) reproduced **91.8%** of the gain (r = 0.917640706647481, ATTENUATION_SUPPORTED), including ~97% of the head-gate rebalancing.

4. **The damping benefit is seed-replicated but does not persist at a longer budget and does not transfer across datasets.**
   - Seed-2 replication (`f589e61`): Δtest **+0.007713701281711671**, Δval **+0.004483122552346286** → STABILITY_SUPPORTED; pooled STABLE (mean Δ +0.0102100/+0.0088812). Magnitude seed-sensitive (0.61× of seed-1 on test).
   - 10-epoch longer budget (`b515f8c`): Δtest **+0.0005273306552253665** < +0.0055 and Δval **−0.002653370173158476** → **NOT_PERSIST**; per-epoch Δ turns negative from ep7 (baseline catches up) — consistent with early-convergence acceleration rather than durable lift. Note the same 10-epoch baseline run also serves the residual-prompt line (bit-equal to its fresh rerun).
   - Cross-dataset transfer (`b990e11`): the constant applied unchanged to CensusIncome gave Δtest **−0.0010749613105119904** (Δval −0.000969247326740863; r = **−0.8292557208445087**, direction reversed; the head-gate rebalancing did not occur) → TRANSFER_NOT_SUPPORTED. The attenuation finding is **AliCCP-specific**.
   - Learnable discoverability (`ac26eea`): a single identity-initialized kink-clamp scalar could not find the operating point (IDENTITY_PRESERVED; transient 0.55% depth for 144 steps then parked at the clamp; Δtest +0.00083).

5. **Residual prompt is the only line whose committed evidence supports mechanism + seed replication + longer-budget persistence — on AliCCP only.**
   - Mechanism (norm-controlled, bounded, zero-init scalar gate on the three pre-fusion streams): M0+G1–G8 all PASS on both seeds (norm bound `ratio ≡ g_eff ≤ |α|` structural and measured; params 2385/8129).
   - Seed 1 (`3e2f083`): VALID_NEGATIVE — Δtest **+0.005394543882337954**, missing the U1 bar by **1.05e-4**; Δval +0.00448174078674779.
   - Seed 2 (`a40836e`): VALID_POSITIVE — Δtest **+0.008103113327467715** (U1 PASS), Δval **+0.008622497456618139** (U2 PASS); pooled **REPLICATION_SUPPORTED**, signs +/+ (descriptive means +0.0067488/+0.0065521).
   - 10-epoch longer budget (`e46e5d2`): **PERSISTS** — Δtest **+0.0074396653390377265**, Δval **+0.0048363447472773435**; C3a/C3b(11/11)/C3c all PASS; independent verifier 47/47 ALL_PASS. Δ narrows monotonically from the ep4 peak (+0.00892) to ep10 (+0.00484) but stays above +0.0055; both arms right-censored at ep10.
   - Caveats that stay attached: **AliCCP-only** (Census counterpart was VALID_NEGATIVE at −0.00233); mechanism internals are seed-unstable (α sign flips −0.0605 → +0.0710 → +0.1026; cos structure +0.36/+0.48/+0.47 → ~0); A3 reruns remain formally SKIP (de-facto bit-equality observed); the contradiction with the attenuation line (same artifact/budget: PERSISTS vs NOT_PERSIST) is itself a recorded observation, not a resolution.

6. **The stage-1 clustering repair is a real mechanism fix, not a utility lever.** Rank/quantile normalization of the per-task cluster losses repaired B4 (env shares 47.96/52.04, then 55.31/44.69 on seed 2; stage-1 cluster events bit-exactly predicted) and removed the "cluster = CVR label function" degeneracy. ## 5. Causal narrative

**CensusIncome (head-side screening).** The protocol branch fixed the fairness substrate (split/model/env seeds decoupled; content-addressed stage-1; true freeze; A/B gates) and produced the single baseline row every later arm cites. Ten arms then probed distinct head-side modifications under a frozen bar (≥ baseline + 0.00203, declared before each run): attention-derived env vectors, a null candidate, conditional scale+bias (with a zero-training router probe), two CGR variants, two affinity variants, a LoRA-style residual, a residual prompt, and a gradient-conflict audit. The pattern was uniform: mechanisms that activate do not move AUC (best +0.0013); mechanisms from the archive are structurally degenerate; the audit finds no action. The one cross-lineage test (importing the AliCCP damping constant) moves the metric the wrong way and lacks the AliCCP mechanism signature. Net: the family's negative is *informative* (mechanism-level, pre-registered, and falsifiable), but it is a stop under this protocol, seed and budget.

**AliCCP (attribution → replication → budget).** The null expert produced a split verdict — utility passed, mechanism failed — which is exactly the case that demands attribution before belief. The constant-damping control explained 91.8% of the gain and, unusually, reproduced the induced head-gate rebalancing. The learnable-scalar branch then showed the training objective itself does **not** discover that operating point from identity (transient 0.55% depth, then parked), implying the benefit requires the specific damping magnitude, not merely "any attenuation". Seed-2 replication held with unrelaxed thresholds (pooled STABLE). The 10-epoch test then separated durable lift from acceleration: per-epoch Δ shrank monotonically and turned negative at ep7 → NOT_PERSIST. In parallel, the clustering repair turned the B4 gate from structural failure to bit-predictable balance on two seeds, but its stage-2 utility direction was seed-unstable (NOT_SUPPORTED). The residual-prompt line repeated this ladder but with a different ending: seed 1 barely under the U1 bar (VALID_NEGATIVE), seed 2 clearly over (REPLICATION_SUPPORTED, but with sign-flipped α and near-zero cos — mechanism path unstable, utility sign stable), and the 10-epoch budget retained the delta (PERSISTS, narrowing from ep4). The divergence between the two AliCCP lines at the same budget/same artifact (attenuation NOT_PERSIST vs residual prompt PERSISTS) is a recorded empirical fact of this screening; it is not explained by the committed evidence.

**Cross-dataset reading.** The only two direct cross-dataset transports both went to CensusIncome: the attenuation constant (negative, r reversed) and the residual-prompt form (VALID_NEGATIVE there; its AliCCP runs are positive). The null-expert mechanism replicated neither cleanly (each dataset passed exactly one of the two gates). Conclusion: nothing here supports cross-dataset generality claims; AliCCP findings stay AliCCP-specific.

## 6. Novelty and prior-art caution

- **No novelty is claimed anywhere in this synthesis, and none should be inferred from the branch docs.** They repeatedly self-declare: cond-scale-bias ("FiLM/条件化调制…不主张任何方法新颖性"), CGR and affinity ("已有大类"), LoRA ("没有任何新意"; explicit bibliography), residual prompt ("构件没有一个是新的"; weak-redundancy vs lora explicitly disclosed), gradient audit ("PCGrad/GradNorm/…已成型文献"), norm clustering ("rank/quantile…标准统计工具").
- **Overlap map**: dynamic/context-generated prompts → prompt tuning, visual prompt tuning, L2P; conditional modulation → FiLM, conditional normalization; residual low-rank adaptation → residual adapters, Houlsby adapters, LoRA, unified PEFT view, AdapterFusion/MAD-X, Ladder Side-Tuning; per-sample gating/abstention and hard routing → MoE gating, Gumbel-softmax routing, conditional computation; gradient surgery → PCGrad/GradNorm/CAGrad/Gradient Vaccine; task weighting → homoscedastic uncertainty weighting.
- **Caution for reuse**: the reference lists in the branch docs were compiled without a systematic literature search (several docs say so explicitly). Treat them as pointers, not verified citations, and re-verify before any write-up. Also note the screening protocol is single-seed, single-run per configuration (A3 SKIP), short-budget, and pinned to one prefix/dataset per lineage — none of it is publication-grade evidence by itself.
